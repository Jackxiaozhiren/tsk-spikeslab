## 2. 新 Abstract（Farquhar 五句式，~156 词 ≤200）

> Calibrated prediction intervals are essential for decision-making under uncertainty, and Bayesian model selection is a principled route to them for rule-based regressions with linear consequents. However, replacing posterior sampling with an analytical approximation—BIC-based inclusion probabilities, hard thresholding, and a Laplace predictive covariance—can collapse both accuracy and coverage on ill-conditioned design matrices. We introduce exact inference for spike-and-slab priors over Takagi–Sugeno–Kang (TSK) consequents: a block-Gibbs sampler whose per-rule inclusion full conditional is computed in $\mathcal{O}(n(d{+}1)^2)$ time via matrix-determinant and Woodbury identities, combined with Bayesian model averaging (BMA) that propagates model uncertainty into the predictive variance. On two UCI benchmarks (three targets), this yields well-calibrated 95\% intervals (PICP $=0.94$--$0.95$) where the analytical approximation undercovers (PICP $=0.00$--$0.18$) at comparable width, and a corrected dense baseline—a membership-function fix raising $R^2$ from $0.41$ to $0.94$ on the energy-cooling target—anchors the comparison. The approach applies to any rule-based regression with linear consequents, and the code and corrected baselines are released for reproducible, uncertainty-aware inference.

**改动点对照**（对应 P1 的 D2/D3 + L-1/L-3）：
- 首句：一般性 UQ 声明（不再是 "TSK fuzzy systems…"）
- BIC+Laplace 改为 "an analytical approximation"（不再 "recent work"，解决 F-1）
- bug 修复压缩为一句并精确到 "energy-cooling target"（解决 L-3）
- 删 "catastrophically"、"at comparable interval width on the Energy targets"
- 尾句 "applies to"（解决 L-4 的 "generalizes to" 过度声明）
- "no free lunch"负结果**完全移出摘要**

## 3. 新贡献段（3 点，方法优先 + 正向前向化）

1. **Exact spike-and-slab inference for linear-consequent rule models.** A block-Gibbs sampler whose per-rule inclusion full conditional is computed in $\mathcal{O}(n(d+1)^2)$ time via matrix-determinant and Woodbury identities, with Bayesian model averaging (BMA) that propagates model uncertainty into the predictive variance.
2. **Calibrated model-averaged prediction intervals and a diagnostic of analytical approximations.** Gibbs sampling with BMA recovers well-calibrated 95\% intervals (PICP $=0.94$--$0.95$), in contrast to a BIC-plus-Laplace approximation that undercovers (PICP $=0.00$--$0.18$); an ablation isolates the failure to hard thresholding and the Laplace covariance collapse.
3. **A sparsity design criterion.** We delineate the regime in which sparse selection is warranted: on low-dimensional, well-conditioned data ($d=8$) neither rule-level nor coefficient-level sparsity beats the dense baseline, indicating that sparsity-inducing priors earn their cost only in high-dimensional or irrelevant-feature regimes (Section~\ref{sec:sparse}).

**改动点对照**（对应 P1 的 H-2/D6 + S4/S5）：
- 贡献 1 从 "TSK consequents" 泛化为 "linear-consequent rule models"（呼应 generality）
- 贡献 3 从 "A verified dense baseline and a sparsity boundary"（纠错+负结果捆绑）改为 "A sparsity design criterion"（正向设计准则），删 "no free lunch"
- bug 修复不再占贡献主位（降为正文/Abstract 一句）

## 4. Scope-fit 论证段落（可复用：摘要尾 + 引言贡献段 + cover letter）

> This paper contributes a general methodology for **calibrated Bayesian inference under model uncertainty**: exact block-Gibbs sampling for spike-and-slab priors combined with Bayesian model averaging, instantiated on Takagi–Sugeno–Kang fuzzy systems. The central object—an exact sampler for linear-consequent rule models whose inclusion probabilities are computed in $\mathcal{O}(n(d{+}1)^2)$ time, together with a model-averaged predictive distribution—is not tied to building-energy or to fuzzy systems specifically. It addresses a question of direct interest to the computational-intelligence and uncertainty-quantification community: **when are analytical approximations to Bayesian model selection (BIC plus Laplace) safe, and when do they miscalibrate prediction intervals?** The primary contribution is therefore a principled uncertainty-quantification method (exact posterior inference + calibrated model-averaged prediction intervals) with a transferable diagnostic, not a single-domain soft-computing application; the reproducibility correction and the sparsity boundary serve as empirical validation.

**这条是 C-1/C-2 的文本解药**：把"精确 vs 近似"的诊断升为一般性问题，TSK/建筑能耗降为验证载体。

## 5. C-2 归因改写（引言 L46，before/after）

**Before（现状，引用错位 + 自指稻草人）：**
> "Recent work proposed spike-and-slab priors \cite{mitchell1988bayesian,george1993variable} for TSK rule selection, but used an analytical approximation, a BIC-based estimate of posterior inclusion probabilities followed by hard thresholding and a Laplace approximation to the predictive covariance, to avoid Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)."

> "Spike-and-slab priors \cite{mitchell1988bayesian,george1993variable} place positive probability on exact zero coefficients. A natural alternative to MCMC is analytical model selection—BIC-based inclusion probabilities followed by hard thresholding and a Laplace approximation to the predictive covariance—which we construct as a reference baseline. On the TSK design matrix this approximation fails in two ways."

**配套修改（cover letter）**：
- 删 "the **first** exact Bayesian inference procedure" → "we present exact Bayesian inference for spike-and-slab priors"
