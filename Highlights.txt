Manuscript: Correctness and Predictive Uncertainty in Bayesian TSK Fuzzy Regression: A Reproducible Evaluation of Spike-and-Slab Model Averaging

- Rebuilds a Bayesian TSK regression pipeline with explicit implementation invariants.
- Compares dense, BIC-based, Gibbs/BMA, Gaussian-process, and conformal intervals.
- Reports near-nominal empirical coverage without claiming universal calibration gains.
- Shows that the earlier BIC collapse is not reproduced after correction.
- Documents sparsity sensitivity, finite-chain diagnostics, and protocol limits.
