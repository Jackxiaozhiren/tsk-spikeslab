\noindent\textbf{Manuscript:} Correctness and Predictive Uncertainty in Bayesian TSK Fuzzy Regression: A Reproducible Evaluation of Spike-and-Slab Model Averaging

\vspace{1.5em}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, labelsep=0.5em]
\item Rebuilds a Bayesian TSK regression pipeline with explicit implementation invariants.
\item Compares dense, BIC-based, Gibbs/BMA, Gaussian-process, and conformal intervals.
\item Reports near-nominal empirical coverage without claiming universal calibration gains.
\item Shows that the earlier BIC collapse is not reproduced after correction.
\item Documents sparsity sensitivity, finite-chain diagnostics, and protocol limits.
\end{itemize}
