% Highlights for: Sparse Bayesian TSK with Spike-and-Slab Priors
% Target: Applied Soft Computing (Elsevier)
% Format: 3-5 bullets, each <=85 characters including spaces

\begin{highlights}
\item Introduces spike-and-slab priors for TSK fuzzy rule consequent selection.
\item Fast analytical BIC approximation selects rules without MCMC sampling.
\item 38\% rule pruning on Energy data; 0\% pruning on Concrete (data-dependent).
\item Bayesian TSK yields calibrated prediction intervals (PICP 0.90--0.96).
\item Full sparsity-accuracy-calibration trade-off characterized on three benchmarks.
\end{highlights}
